# Discovery Framework

How "find me opportunities" becomes a defensible ranked list.

## Signal families

Eight families, one convergence test. A family only counts when its
evidence is current, attributable to a Lexfi call, and independent of the
other counted families (two sentiment tools agreeing = one family).

| Family | Equities evidence | Crypto evidence | FX evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price / market action | movers, sector performance, price history | coin markets, dominance, market chart | pair history, DXY |
| Fundamentals | key metrics, statements, estimates | TVL, adoption proxies | macro data momentum |
| News / sentiment | stocks news sentiment, CNN fear/greed | crypto news sentiment, crypto fear/greed | forex sentiment, AI FX ratio |
| Earnings / management | surprises, call insights (Pro Max) | — (protocol events under catalysts) | CB tone (cb_insights) |
| Positioning / flows | insider, congress, 13F, superinvestors | funding, OI, liquidations, whales, exchange flows, crypto ETF flows | rate probabilities vs pricing |
| Macro | regime, rates, liquidity | liquidity, dollar regime | rate differentials, forecasts |
| Catalysts | earnings dates, events, M&A/IPO | protocol/policy events, prediction markets | CB meetings, data calendar |
| Alternative data | prediction markets, social highlights | social + telegram highlights | prediction markets |

## The convergence rule

```text
OPPORTUNITY = (≥2 independent families aligned, current)
            + (identifiable catalyst or mechanism)
            − (strong opposing family caps conviction at Medium)
```

Conviction: **Low** = 2 families; **Medium** = 3+, or any strong opposition;
**High** = 3+ aligned, catalyst present, no strong opposition. Never higher.

Divergence is the mirror image and equally valuable: families in
*disagreement* (price vs information) surface in divergence-discovery,
where the boring explanations — already priced, stale leg, thin data — are
tested before the exciting one.

## The funnel (cost architecture)

```text
BROAD (5–6 calls, whole market):   snapshot tools only — movers, sectors,
                                   coin markets, global crypto, DXY
   ↓ nominate 10–20 candidates (movers, laggards-in-moving-sectors,
     rotation seams, dominance shifts)
SIGNAL (4–7 calls, cross-universe): funding/OI/liquidations (all coins in
                                   one call each), aggregate sentiment,
                                   fear/greed pair, AI FX ratio
   ↓ score families per candidate; keep 3–6
DEPTH (1–2 calls each):            targeted news / positioning / calendar
                                   on the shortlist only
```

Never invert the funnel. Never deep-dive to "check one more thing" on a
candidate that failed the signal layer.

## Ranking output obligations

Per surfaced candidate: why it surfaced (family: evidence bullets), the
strongest opposing signal, catalyst, working thesis [interpretation],
invalidation, asset-appropriate risks. Plus, for the scan as a whole:
"not surfaced but considered" (proves a funnel ran) and a sources/as-of
line naming gaps.

## Modes

| Mode | Owner skill | Twist |
|---|---|---|
| General | opportunity-discovery | balanced weights |
| Momentum | opportunity-discovery | require price AND information momentum both accelerating |
| Theme | opportunity-discovery | ≥3 related assets sharing aligned families; name the theme |
| Class-scoped | stock/crypto/fx-discovery | class-specific family depth |
| Catalyst | catalyst-discovery | time-anchored; impact × direction × timing |
| Divergence | divergence-discovery | disagreement, both legs current |
| Macro-driven | macro-impact-analysis | start from scenario, walk transmission chains |
