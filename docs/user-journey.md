# User Journey

The product loop, walked end-to-end. Each stage names its skill, its
entry/exit, and what carries forward.

```text
DISCOVER → WHY? → RESEARCH → CHALLENGE → THESIS → RISK → MONITOR
   ↑                                                        │
   └────────────────── "what changed?" ─────────────────────┘
```

## 1. DISCOVER — "Find me something interesting"

**Skills:** opportunity-discovery (cross-asset), stock/crypto/fx-discovery
(scoped), catalyst-discovery, divergence-discovery.
**In:** nothing, or loose criteria. **Out:** 3–10 ranked candidates, each
with named converging signals, the strongest opposing signal, a catalyst,
a working thesis line, and an invalidation.
**Honesty valve:** "no strong convergence today" is a valid, delivered
answer.

## 2. WHY? — "Why did this surface / why did it move?"

**Skill:** asset-deep-dive (fast mode, 2–4 calls).
The bridge stage: before committing research effort, the user checks the
mechanism. Exit: either drop the candidate or promote it to full research.

## 3. RESEARCH — "Research this properly"

**Skills:** asset-deep-dive (full), earnings-analysis,
crypto-intelligence, fx-macro-analysis.
**In:** one asset (+ the discovery entry if it exists — not re-fetched).
**Out:** a dossier ending in explicit FACT / SIGNAL / INTERPRETATION
sections — the raw material of a thesis, kept on the evidence ladder.

## 4. CHALLENGE — "What am I missing?"

**Skill:** thesis-challenger.
Deliberately placed **before** thesis formalization when entered from
research ("challenge this idea"), and available again after (challenge the
written thesis). Adversarial retrieval: contrary sentiment trend, crowding,
valuation, macro exposure, smart-money behavior, event risk. Out: graded
challenges (Serious/Notable/Minor) + verdict. A Serious unanswered
challenge should kill or reshape the idea — that is the stage working, not
failing.

## 5. THESIS — "Write it down so it can be wrong"

**Skill:** thesis-builder (incl. bull/base/bear mode).
**In:** the research ladder + surviving challenges. **Out:** the thesis
contract — direction, horizon, evidence, catalysts, counter-thesis, risks,
**invalidation conditions**, confidence with its limiter. The invalidation
section is the product: it converts opinion into something monitorable.

## 6. RISK — "What does being wrong cost?"

**Skill:** risk-reward-analysis.
**In:** the thesis. **Out:** scenario ranges anchored to realized
volatility, asymmetry assessment, crowding/event-risk checks, invalidation
levels, sizing *frameworks* (never prescriptions). Exit: the user decides;
the system never does.

## 7. MONITOR — "What's changed?"

**Skill:** watchlist-monitor.
**In:** watchlist + any stored theses/invalidations. **Out:** deltas only —
price direction, information trend, sentiment, catalysts ahead, and
**thesis status: Strengthening / Weakening / Unchanged**, including "an
invalidation condition has triggered" as a first-class alert. Loop closure:
a triggered invalidation or a new catalyst feeds back into DISCOVER/WHY.

## Ambient stages

`market-regime` and `macro-impact-analysis` aren't loop stages — they are
context the user consults at any stage ("does the regime even favor this
kind of trade?"), and discovery consumes their read implicitly through its
broad layer.

## Journey properties

- **Enter anywhere.** "Challenge my NVDA thesis" starts at stage 4; the
  skill asks for the thesis, not for the user to restart the loop.
- **Context carries.** Later stages reuse in-conversation evidence; calls
  are for gaps and freshness only.
- **Every stage can say no.** Weak convergence, broken thesis, ugly
  risk/reward — the loop is designed to filter ideas out, because the
  user's scarce resources are capital and attention, not ideas.
